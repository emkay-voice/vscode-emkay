# Your first voice skill

1. **Make a skill** – a few small files: the sentences you say (`.intent`), what Emkay answers (`.dialog`), and a little Python.
2. **Run it** – Emkay opens next to your code with your skill in it.
3. **Talk to it** – type what you would say under Emkay.
4. **Test it** – your golden utterances, ✓ or ✗, in the Testing view.

More: [dev.emkay.ai](https://dev.emkay.ai)
